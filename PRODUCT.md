# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16, React 19, Tailwind CSS 4, TypeScript, Biome

## Users

Recruiters, creative directors, potential collaborators, and clients evaluating Sahas for design or full-stack development work. They are time-constrained professionals scanning for signal — they need to quickly assess craft, taste, and technical range.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio website that showcases Sahas's work as a designer who also builds. The site should make visitors believe Sahas is a high-caliber full-stack builder with sharp design sensibility — someone who ships, not someone who just mocks up.

## Positioning

Design-plus-code hybrid: unusually strong at both visual design and engineering execution. A single person who can take a product from concept to deployed code, and make it look good doing it. This combination is rare enough to be the defining differentiator.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from job applications, social links, or word-of-mouth. They spend 30–90 seconds on a first scan, then decide whether to dig deeper or leave. The site must survive that scan. Key actions: viewing project case studies, checking technical skills, reaching out via contact info or social links.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Project showcase with case studies (content to be created)
- About/bio section
- Skills or capabilities display
- Contact links (email, GitHub, LinkedIn, etc.)
- Responsive across desktop and mobile
- Fast, accessible, and performant — no heavy client-side JS
- No analytics or tracking without explicit consent later
- Dark/light mode support expected from the existing Tailwind setup

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Sahas
- Voice: confident, direct, not boastful — let the work speak
- Existing design.json references a prior "Appniche Technologies" system with navy/blue palette; this may or may not be Sahas's brand — treat as reference, not binding

## Evidence on Hand

- None yet. No photos, project screenshots, case study copy, testimonials, or resume data exist. Future work must not fabricate these — placeholders or skeleton content only until provided.

## Product Principles

1. **Work-first hierarchy** — project examples and case studies are the primary content; everything else supports them.
2. **Brevity earns attention** — every section should be as short as it can be while still being complete.
3. **Craft is the evidence** — the portfolio's own design quality is a direct proof point for Sahas's capabilities.
4. **No decoration without function** — every visual element must serve comprehension or navigation.
5. **Mobile-first credibility** — the site must look equally considered on a phone as on a wide monitor.
